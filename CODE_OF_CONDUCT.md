# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TurboPDF welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity,
experience level, background or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and precise.
- Welcome questions and differing technical viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers through a GitHub security advisory. Maintainers may
remove comments, reject contributions or temporarily or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
